Pint's Auctions - Changelog

v1.2.14
- Writ tooltips show the profession and skill that makes the requested item (green if you can make it, red if
  your skill is too low, grey if you don't have the profession), and the item's own tooltip appears beside it.
- WoW Forever: an X on the Pint's Auctions panel closes it, and a Pint's button on the auction house title bar shows or
  hides it (remembered).

v1.2.13
- First CurseForge release. Auction house prices from full scans (in every tooltip), shopping lists, selling,
  undercut cancelling and crafting costs; answers Auctionator's price API.
- Crates and Writs: turn-in NPC and zone for each writ, a Turn-ins view (foldable, nearest first), a Route view (best
  order, with the mailbox items for each stop), map pins, writ tooltips, a pop-out route tracker (/pah route), and the
  Durotar Supply and Logistics window.
- Full scans price Pint's Professions' levelling guides and Flip.
