Pint's Auctions - auction house prices, shopping, selling and crafting costs
(WoW Classic Era / WoW Forever)

REQUIRES
  Nothing. Standalone. Replaces Auctionator, and answers Auctionator's price API so add-ons that read its prices
  keep working.

INSTALL
  Unzip so the folder sits at:  World of Warcraft\_classic_era_\Interface\AddOns\PintsAuctions
  (or _forever_ for WoW Forever). Restart the game.

WHAT IT DOES
  Full scans save a price for every item on your server, shown in item tooltips everywhere. Shopping lists find
  what you need at the best price; the Sell tab prices your items against the market; Cancel finds your auctions
  that have been undercut so you can repost them; recipes show what they cost to craft at today's prices.
  Crates and Writs: crafting crates and writs with their turn-in NPCs, a Turn-ins list, a best-order Route with the
  mailbox items you need at each stop, map pins and a pop-out route tracker (/pah route).
  Its full scans also price Pint's Professions (levelling guides and Flip).

  /pah - options
